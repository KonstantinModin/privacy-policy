# Child Safety Standards Our app has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We strictly prohibit: * child sexual abuse material (CSAM) * grooming or exploitation of minors * any sexual content involving minors * trafficking or abuse of children Users can report inappropriate content or behavior through the app support contact. We comply with applicable child safety laws and cooperate with law enforcement when required.
