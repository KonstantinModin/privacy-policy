Child Safety Standards

Our application has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following are strictly prohibited on our platform:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Any sexual content involving minors
  • Child exploitation or trafficking
  • Grooming or predatory behavior
  • Harassment or abuse targeting minors

Enforcement

Any violation of these standards may result in immediate account suspension, content removal, and reporting to relevant law enforcement authorities.

Reporting

Users can report inappropriate behavior or content through our support contact. We review reports promptly and take appropriate action.

Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws and Google Play policies regarding child safety and prevention of child sexual abuse and exploitation.
